Welcome to Inkframe = 
A quick look around the window: seven stops, under a minute. Press Enter or → to go on, and Esc to leave the tour at any time. = 
The tools = 
Drawing, painting, rigging and the camera. Hover over a tool to see what it does. A small triangle means other tools share its slot: right-click it, or press the tool's key with Shift. = 
What you can do now = 
This bar follows what you're doing: the tool you have, and buttons for what can be done with what's selected, such as making a symbol or bending a drawing. Its right end switches rulers, onion skins and the light table. = 
The stage = 
Your picture. What's inside the frame is what gets exported. Scroll to pan, pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom, and ⌘0 fits it in view again. = 
Properties = 
Settings for what you're working on: the current Tool, the selected Layer, and the whole Document. Every area's name is a menu: click it to show something else there, such as the Library. = 
The timeline = 
Your layers, and their keyframes over time. + adds a layer to draw into. Move to another frame and change a layer, and it's keyed there. Enter plays. = 
Find anything = 
Can't find a command? Press ⌘K and type its name, or what it's for: "bulge", "rig", "web page". It also shows which menu it's in and its keys. = 
Learning more = 
Help has Inkframe for Artists, a beginner's guidebook with a chapter for each group of tools, and this tour again. File › Open Example has finished projects to take apart. Have fun! =
